      Questions

      What parents and teachers ask us most.

      Email us

      Who are the stories for?

      Children who are starting to read, about 4 to 8 years old, alone or with a grown-up.

      Where do the stories come from?

      They are old tales that are free for anyone to share, like Aesop's fables and Indonesian folk tales. We tell them again in simple words.

      How long is each story?

      Six short pages, with one or two sentences on each. Most children read one in about five minutes.

      How does the story voice work?

      Read to me uses the voice on your phone or computer. Recorded voices are coming with the full library.

      Is the daily story free?

      Yes. One story is free every day. The full library will be a paid plan.

      Do kids need an account to read?

      No. Open the page and read. There is nothing to sign up for.
